# AOC-001 — Consequence Entry Mechanism v0.1

**Status:** Evidence-tested story mechanism. Suitable for fiction development, with causal boundary preserved.

## Finding

The strongest first mechanism is **not** "AI creates a new mine job for Mauricio."

That causal claim cannot currently be supported.

Instead, AOC-001 should use a two-layer structure:

### Reader-visible global layer
AI/data-centre expansion is increasing electricity-system and material requirements. The IEA identifies AI/data-centre growth as a source of renewed electricity demand and pressure on grid-component and critical-material supply chains, including copper.

### Family-visible local layer
Mauricio's world is already experiencing real copper-sector investment, project continuity, contracting and labour demand in the Antofagasta Region.

In 2026:
- Chuquicamata Underground's Level 1 continuity infrastructure was reported 94% complete.
- Codelco's awarded-contract register shows multi-year construction, engineering, maintenance and operational contracts at Chuquicamata.
- The Antofagasta regional government/mining sector created an employment coordination effort specifically aimed at increasing local hiring around mining operations and investment projects.
- Elsewhere in the region, Codelco and Pucobre advanced Tovaku, a US$870m project intended to add 46,000 tonnes of annual copper cathode production toward 2030.
- Ministro Hales received approval for a US$2.8bn project extending operations to 2054 and increasing planned annual output.

These developments have multiple causes. Atlas must not attribute them to AI.

## Story mechanism

**Opportunity, not causation.**

Mauricio is offered or becomes eligible for a better-paid/longer-duration contract, additional responsibility, specialist training, or a roster-related opportunity connected to the existing regional mining/project environment.

The exact opportunity should be fictional unless tied to a documented real program.

The reader, meanwhile, has already been shown that AI/data-centre growth is one additional source of pressure on global electricity and copper systems.

This creates the Atlas consequence without falsely claiming:

AI → Mauricio's contract.

Instead:

AI is one growing participant in global copper demand
+
Antofagasta is a major copper-producing region already expanding/maintaining production
+
Mauricio's household lives inside that copper economy.

The fiction asks what stronger or more persistent copper demand **could** mean if it reinforces the economic incentives already surrounding families like theirs.

## Why this is stronger dramatically

The opportunity should initially look good.

More money, greater job security, useful training, or a stronger career path gives Mauricio a rational reason to want it.

The cost should emerge from the conditions attached to the opportunity:
- roster;
- longer absences;
- changed responsibilities;
- a decision about where the family lives;
- pressure on Daniela's routines;
- Tomás seeing mining as an increasingly obvious career path.

This preserves moral complexity. Global demand does not arrive as a villain. It arrives as **an opportunity with consequences**.

## First story pressure

Recommended v0.1 pressure:

**Mauricio is offered a step forward in his mining-contracting career that improves the household's financial outlook but makes the work/home balance harder.**

Do not yet lock:
- employer;
- exact contract;
- salary;
- roster;
- mine;
- training program;
- claim that AI caused the opportunity.

Those details require the next factual/authenticity pass.

## Fact / fiction separation

### WHAT'S REAL
- AI/data centres increase electricity demand and require material-intensive infrastructure.
- Copper is important to data centres and grid expansion.
- Antofagasta is a major copper region.
- Major copper investment and continuity projects are active in the region.
- Mining contracting and local-employment initiatives are real.
- Mining rosters and commuting can affect family life.

### FICTIONAL BRIDGE
- Mauricio.
- His employer.
- His particular opportunity.
- His household's response.
- Any connection between changing global copper demand and his individual employment conditions.

### QUESTION AT THE HEART OF THE EDITION
If distant technological demand strengthens the value of what your region produces, what happens when the opportunity it creates asks more of the people who produce it?
