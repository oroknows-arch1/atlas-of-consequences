## Factual short caption

AI can feel weightless. The infrastructure carrying it is not.

The IEA estimates data centres used about **415 TWh of electricity in 2024**, around **1.5% of global electricity consumption**, and projects data-centre electricity use to more than double by 2030 in its base case. That growth adds to demand for generation, grids and materials including copper.

AOC-001 follows that documented physical chain into northern Chile, then clearly changes mode before exploring a fictional family's human consequences.

**This edition does not claim that AI caused a specific Antofagasta mine expansion or an individual job.**

Read the complete edition: https://atlas-of-consequences.onrender.com/

Evidence anchors: SR-01, SR-02, SR-04, SR-05, SR-07, SR-08.

## Factual compact caption

AI is digital activity carried by physical systems. Data centres used an estimated **415 TWh of electricity in 2024**. AOC-001 follows the infrastructure chain through electricity, copper and northern Chile — without claiming that AI caused a specific mine expansion or job.

Read the full sourced edition: https://atlas-of-consequences.onrender.com/

## Description

A sourced Atlas report on the physical systems behind AI growth: data centres, electricity, grids, copper and the connection to Chile's Antofagasta Region and Calama. The report is followed in the full edition by a separately labelled work of fiction exploring possible human consequences without presenting the fictional family as evidence.
