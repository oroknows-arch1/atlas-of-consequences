# Atlas Reader Architecture v0.3 — Progressive Perspective Reader

**Status:** human-approved direction / implementation test
**Test edition:** AOC-001 — *AI's Physical Hunger*
**Decision date:** 2026-09-26

## Core change

Atlas no longer treats a complete linear edition or generated fictional video as the default delivery unit.

The reader establishes the documented reality first, then lets the user choose where they are standing relative to that change. Atlas progressively opens the relevant consequence path while deeper context, the complete edition and sources remain available on demand.

**LIGHT ENTRY → WHAT'S REAL → PERSPECTIVE CHOICE → RELEVANT CONSEQUENCE PATH → OPTIONAL DEPTH → SOURCES**

## Perspective principle

The same documented change can matter differently from different positions. A perspective changes the route through the edition; it does not change the underlying evidence.

AOC-001 test perspectives:
- **Family + labour** — household, work, absence, skills and opportunity.
- **Markets + finance** — demand, constraints, supply, capital and commercial interpretation.
- **Land + place** — infrastructure, extraction, water, territory and local pressure.
- **Culture + community** — identity, community continuity and documented cultural boundaries.
- **Technology + infrastructure** — compute, data centres, electricity, grids and physical materials.

Only evidence-supported paths may be exposed. A missing perspective is preferable to invented relevance.

## Progressive disclosure

The opening experience should be lighter than the v0.2 full-edition map. The reader should not be required to consume every section in sequence.

1. Establish the real change and its evidence boundary.
2. Ask: **Where are you standing?**
3. Reorder/highlight the most relevant existing material for that perspective.
4. Keep the full edition, PLACE and SOURCES available.
5. Preserve a clear route back to the common factual spine.

Perspective selection is navigation, not personal profiling. It need not infer sensitive attributes or permanently identify the reader.

## Fiction

Fiction remains an optional explanatory/human layer. It must stay visibly fictional and may be used where it materially improves understanding.

A fictional story is not mandatory for every perspective and is never evidence.

## Media rule

Generated fictional video is removed from the definition of a complete Atlas edition.

Video is an optional enhancement selected only when expected editorial/distribution value justifies generation cost, latency and QA burden.

No edition may be blocked because a generated fictional video does not exist.

The existing AOC-001 factual opening film remains a reusable test asset; it is not a template requirement for future editions.

## Protected invariants

- Facts can change the fiction. Fiction must never quietly become fact.
- Material factual claims remain traceable to evidence.
- Connection is not silently upgraded to causation.
- Fiction cannot become market/investment evidence.
- PLACE and cultural/Indigenous boundaries remain explicit.
- SOURCES remain inspectable.
- The full canonical edition remains accessible regardless of perspective.

## Scale rule

Future EMRADAR → Atlas validation runs must be able to complete without paid generative video. Media enhancement sits downstream of a complete edition and may be skipped.

## AOC-001 migration test

AOC-001 preserves its locked content and evidence. v0.3 changes presentation and routing only.

Success means a mobile reader can:
1. understand the real change quickly;
2. select a perspective;
3. receive a visibly relevant route without losing evidence boundaries;
4. change perspective without restarting the edition;
5. open the full edition and source register;
6. complete the experience with no newly generated video.
