**Ecosystem:** AOC-ECO-0001  
**Status:** Prototype / research in progress  
**Publication status:** Not yet ready for publication

## Working consequence chain
Global AI/data-centre expansion
→ electricity and water demand
→ grid/nuclear/infrastructure investment
→ critical-mineral demand such as copper
→ strategic investment/bargaining in producing regions
→ possible employment, migration, housing, community, and geopolitical consequences.

This chain is a research hypothesis, not a claim that every downstream consequence has occurred.

Evidence determines what Atlas may present as fact.
