# AOC-001 — Mauricio Work Authenticity Lock v0.1

**Status:** Evidence-supported fictional design lock.  
**Scope:** Mauricio's occupation, opportunity and roster only.  
**Date:** 2026-09-22.

## Locked character baseline

Mauricio Soto, 40, is a **mining-equipment maintenance technician** employed by a **contractor/service company working on a large mining operation in the Calama area**.

For story purposes he is technically experienced rather than managerial: he diagnoses faults, performs or coordinates preventive/corrective maintenance, works from technical procedures and service records, and has enough experience to be trusted with difficult jobs.

Do not name a real employer or assert that he works for Codelco, Chuquicamata or any other real operation in the fiction unless a later editorial decision deliberately chooses that treatment. Real job advertisements establish plausibility; they are not Mauricio's biography.

## Evidence supporting the occupation

Contemporary employment evidence supports this as an ordinary plausible role in the Calama/Chuquicamata mining system:

- A 2026 Chilean Bolsa Nacional de Empleo listing for an external company sought an electromechanical maintenance technician for preventive, corrective and predictive maintenance, including technical leadership of scheduled work, on 7x7 and 8x6 shifts.
- A recent Chuquicamata maintenance advertisement sought electrical/electronics/automation technicians for preventive and corrective maintenance and fault diagnosis, with 4x3, 7x7 or 14x14 shifts depending on operation.
- Finning advertised technical mechanical work associated with Chuquicamata requiring mining-equipment diagnosis/repair experience and 7x7 rotating day/night shifts.
- Codelco's 2026 Chuquicamata underground maintainer recruitment offered Calama-based 7x7 work, demonstrating that this roster and maintenance occupation exist in the local labour system.

These sources support the **world conditions**. They do not establish that a person like Mauricio is typical, nor that AI demand created any of these jobs.

## Locked fictional opportunity

Mauricio is offered a **step-up maintenance role on a new/extended contractor assignment**.

The fictional step forward gives him:
- greater technical responsibility;
- improved pay;
- a clearer path toward senior technical/lead-maintainer work;
- stronger short-to-medium-term job security.

The role remains hands-on. Mauricio is not suddenly promoted into an executive or office-management position.

The opportunity is deliberately **not** a named real recruitment programme, mine expansion or AI-linked project.

## Locked roster consequence

**Current fictional household rhythm:** Mauricio works an **8x6-style roster** — eight workdays followed by six rest days.

**Opportunity:** the new role moves him to a **7x7 rotating day/night roster**.

At first glance 7x7 does not simply mean "more days away." The story pressure comes from the quality and placement of family time:
- rotating night work makes parts of the home period recovery time rather than ordinary shared time;
- the new technical responsibility makes the roster less psychologically clean at the edges;
- important school/family events can fall entirely inside an away block;
- the family gains a predictable seven-day home block but must reorganise around alternating work rhythms.

This is stronger and more credible than inventing an extreme roster solely to manufacture absence.

**Editorial safeguard:** before prose, verify the exact transition mechanics and whether the chosen fictional 8x6 → rotating 7x7 change genuinely produces the household effects used in scenes. If not, change the fictional starting roster rather than distort real roster behaviour.

## What this changes in the story

Mauricio can plausibly see the offer as progress rather than sacrifice:
- his technical skill is being recognised;
- the household receives a meaningful financial/career benefit;
- at 40, the opportunity can feel like a chance to turn years of experience into a stronger position.

Daniela can agree that it is a good opportunity while seeing consequences Mauricio initially discounts.

The conflict therefore remains:

**This is good news. What does accepting the good news ask from the family?**

No villain is required.

## Causal boundary

### Supported reality

Calama/Chuquicamata's mining labour system contains skilled maintenance roles, contractor/service-company employment, technical progression and roster structures including 7x7, 8x6 and rotating day/night arrangements.

### Fiction

Mauricio Soto, his employer, his existing 8x6 roster, the offer, its compensation, the move to rotating 7x7, and every household response are invented.

### Prohibited claim

Do not write or imply:

**AI growth → a specific Antofagasta mining project → contractor expansion → Mauricio's promotion.**

AOC-001's defensible architecture remains:

**AI/data-centre growth → additional global electricity/grid/material demand pressure → copper is one relevant material → Antofagasta is an existing major copper economy → fiction explores one possible household consequence inside that existing system.**
