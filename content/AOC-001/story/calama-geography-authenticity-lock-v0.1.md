# AOC-001 — Calama / Chuquicamata Scene Geography Lock v0.1

**Status:** Evidence-supported spatial design lock.  
**Scope:** Minimum geography and worker-movement logic required for AOC-001.  
**Date:** 2026-09-22.

## Locked spatial model

AOC-001 treats **Calama as the family's home city** and the mining operations north of the city as a separate work environment.

Chuquicamata is approximately **15 km north of Calama**. Codelco also places Ministro Hales almost 10 km north of Calama on the road toward Chuquicamata. The story therefore must not write the mine as if it sits inside the family's neighbourhood or as if Daniela and the children casually move through the industrial operation.

Mauricio crosses a real boundary between **city/home life** and **faena/work life**.

## Worker movement

Codelco documentation establishes organised personnel transport between Calama and Chuquicamata, including buses/minibuses and shift-related movement. Codelco has also documented electric buses carrying Chuquicamata workers.

This supports a strong fictional default:

**Mauricio uses employer/contractor-organised worker transport for the work leg rather than driving his own car to the mine every shift.**

The exact pickup point, route, operator and journey time remain unlocked. They must not be invented as facts.

This gives the story a useful physical transition: leaving home → joining worker transport → travelling north out of Calama → entering the industrial work system.

## Home geography

Do **not** name a Calama neighbourhood in v0.1.

The household can simply live in Calama in a built-up residential area with plausible access to Daniela's work, school life, shopping and worker transport.

A named barrio adds little to the first story but creates unnecessary authenticity risk.

## Chuquicamata relationship

Modern household life is in Calama, not the former Chuquicamata residential camp.

Codelco documents the historical relocation of Chuquicamata families to Calama. That history may later support an older relative's memory, but it must not be blurred into the contemporary geography.

For the present story:
- **Calama = home/city/family life**
- **Chuquicamata or another northern operation = work/faena**
- **road + organised transport = transition between them**

Mauricio's exact mine remains fictional/unlocked.

## Scene rules

Permitted:
- Mauricio leaving Calama very early or returning after organised shift transport, once exact shift timing is checked;
- a bus/minibus or organised worker transport as part of his transition;
- the physical separation between family home and mine work;
- Daniela and the children remaining in Calama during his work block;
- a visual transition from city to industrial/desert mining landscape without pretending the family lives beside the pit.

Avoid:
- invented exact commute minutes;
- invented bus stops or pickup streets;
- naming a real employer shuttle as Mauricio's service;
- having the family casually enter operational mining areas;
- treating Chuquicamata's former camp as a current residential neighbourhood;
- compressing Calama, Chuquicamata and other mines into one interchangeable place;
- using a specific mine merely because it is famous.

## Story consequence

The geography strengthens the emotional architecture without melodrama.

Mauricio is not necessarily hundreds of kilometres from his family. The important separation is produced by **work access, shift structure and roster rhythm**, not dramatic geographic remoteness.

That distinction matters. A mine can be physically close to Calama while a worker's schedule still makes them unavailable for ordinary household life.

Atlas should let that contradiction stand:

**The work can be nearby on a map and still remove Mauricio from Tuesday.**

## Evidence boundary

### Supported reality

- Chuquicamata is about 15 km north of Calama.
- Ministro Hales is also north of Calama on the corridor toward Chuquicamata.
- Organised personnel transport between Calama and Chuquicamata is documented.
- Codelco has operated worker buses for Chuquicamata.
- The historical Chuquicamata residential relocation to Calama is documented.

### Fiction

Mauricio's worksite, employer, transport provider, pickup location, commute timing, home location and every journey are invented.

### Prohibited inference

Do not treat a Codelco transport system as proof that Mauricio's fictional contractor necessarily uses the same system. It establishes local plausibility only.
