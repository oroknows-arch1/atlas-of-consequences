# AOC-001 — Character Architecture v0.1

**Status:** Fictional household prototype. Characters are invented; locality and work-pattern constraints are informed by the Local Context Pack. Names, occupations and biographical details require a later Chilean/local authenticity pass before publication.

## Design rule

The family must remain interesting if the AI/copper thesis is removed.

Mining is part of their economy, history and household rhythm. It is not their entire identity.

## The household

### Primary viewpoint — Daniela Rojas, 38

Daniela lives in Calama and works in an administrative/support role connected to everyday city life rather than at a mine. The exact employer is intentionally unresolved pending local occupational research.

She is the household's continuity. When her partner is away on shift, school messages, appointments, groceries, relatives, repairs and unexpected problems arrive to her by default. She has become extremely capable at running the house without waiting for another adult.

That capability creates a contradiction when her partner returns: she wants him home, but his return also changes a system she has spent days making work.

Daniela does not think of herself as a victim of mining. The income attached to mining has materially helped the family. She also understands the price of the roster better than anyone.

Her private ambition is not necessarily to leave Calama. It is to reach a point where the family can make decisions because they want to, rather than because a roster or pay packet makes the decision for them.

### Partner — Mauricio Soto, 40

Mauricio works a mining-related roster. For v0.1 he should probably be a skilled contractor/technical worker rather than a senior executive or stereotypical underground miner. Exact role and roster require verification.

He takes pride in being reliable and providing well for his family. He does not romanticise the work, but neither does he resent it.

When he is away, he misses ordinary life. When he comes home, he sometimes discovers that ordinary life has continued without him.

He may attempt to compensate for absence by saying yes—to purchases, outings, takeaway, football, whatever the children want. Daniela sometimes sees this as affection and sometimes as disruption.

His central contradiction:

**The work he does for his family is also the work that repeatedly removes him from it.**

Do not make him emotionally absent simply because he is physically absent. He can be attentive, funny, involved and loving while still failing to know what happened on Tuesday.

### Older child — Tomás, 16

Tomás is old enough to understand what mining wages can provide and old enough to notice what the roster costs.

He should not automatically hate mining or dream of escaping Calama.

His future is genuinely undecided.

A mining-linked technical career could offer him a clear path to strong income close to the world he already understands. Other education or work could pull him elsewhere.

His parents may project different hopes onto that decision without either being obviously right.

Tomás gives AOC-001 a future-facing question:

**If global demand creates more opportunity around copper, does the next generation move toward it—or use the opportunity it created to build something elsewhere?**

### Younger child — Emilia, 7

Emilia is not a symbol and should not deliver thematic dialogue.

Her job in the story is to be seven.

School, friends, snacks, football loyalties, something she wants to show a parent, an argument with her brother, a missing shoe or a bedtime ritual should matter more to her than copper.

Her relationship with Mauricio gives us a place to show the residue of absence through behaviour rather than explanation.

Possible behaviours to explore—not fixed plot points:
- she asks Daniela first even when Mauricio is sitting beside her;
- Mauricio does not know that a routine has changed;
- she saves something to show him when he gets home, then forgets;
- on his first day home he tries to resume an old ritual that the household has quietly outgrown;
- she is delighted when he returns but still treats Daniela as the automatic authority.

Do **not** reproduce the real anecdote that inspired this principle. Its value is the emotional observation, not the incident itself.

## Extended family

At least one nearby relative should exist so the household does not behave like an isolated nuclear-family model.

A grandparent, sibling or cousin can provide ordinary practical support and another view of Calama's changes over time.

A strong possibility is a grandparent with personal memory of the Chuquicamata relocation era. This must be historically plausible by age and chronology and should be verified before locking the biography.

The relative should have their own interests and opinions rather than existing as a history narrator.

## Household rhythm

The story should feel different depending on whether Mauricio is home.

### Away
Daniela runs a stable system. Communication with Mauricio happens, but he cannot participate physically. Decisions sometimes have to be made without him.

### Return
There is genuine happiness. There is also readjustment.

Mauricio has moved from a highly structured work environment into a household whose structure changed while he was gone.

The family does not announce this conflict. It appears through small frictions, jokes, assumptions and routines.

### Before departure
Departure is normal enough that nobody needs a cinematic goodbye every time.

That normality may be more affecting than melodrama.

## Money

The mining-linked income must produce visible benefits.

The family should not be written as secretly destitute merely to manufacture stakes.

Potential benefits:
- greater household stability;
- ability to absorb some unexpected expenses;
- children's activities;
- technology;
- occasional travel or meals out;
- savings or aspirations that would be harder on a lower income.

But income does not automatically settle:
- time;
- parenting presence;
- relationship strain;
- health;
- education choices;
- housing aspirations;
- whether the children stay in Calama.

## Ordinary world

Before AOC-001 enters the plot, scenes should establish:
- breakfast or school preparation;
- shopping and prices;
- neighbourhood interactions;
- family messaging while Mauricio is away;
- football/Cobreloa where natural;
- heat, shade and desert-city practicalities without exoticising them;
- transport;
- Daniela's work;
- Tomás's friends and future plans;
- Emilia being a child;
- Mauricio's transition between faena rhythm and home rhythm.

## The emotional engine

Avoid a simple question such as:

**Is mining good or bad?**

The family carries a harder question:

**What does providing for your family mean when the best way you know to provide also requires repeatedly leaving them?**

That question can exist independently of AI.

AOC-001 later adds pressure to the existing system rather than creating the system from nothing.

## How the global consequence may eventually enter

Do not begin with a news report about AI.

The first consequence should be mundane and locally plausible.

Candidate mechanisms for later evidence testing:
- contractor demand changes;
- a new training opportunity;
- additional shifts/overtime;
- a project extension;
- recruitment;
- a technical specialisation becoming more valuable;
- a decision involving Tomás's education;
- a household financial choice enabled by stronger work prospects.

No mechanism is approved as factual until research connects it credibly to an observed or plausible copper-demand pathway.

## Character authenticity gate

Before publication:
1. verify plausible surnames/naming conventions;
2. verify Mauricio's exact occupation, employer type and roster;
3. verify Daniela's occupation;
4. verify school/family routines;
5. verify neighbourhood geography;
6. verify contemporary local vocabulary and dialogue with Chilean/local sources;
7. verify household economics against plausible occupations;
8. avoid assigning Indigenous, migrant or other identity characteristics without sufficient contextual research.

## Character test

Remove AI, data centres and changing copper demand.

Daniela still has work tomorrow. Mauricio still leaves and returns. Tomás still has to decide what he wants after school. Emilia still wants both parents to notice the things that matter to her. The household still has money decisions, jokes, routines, disagreements, relatives and plans.

**Pass.**

The consequence can now enter their lives without being the reason they exist.
