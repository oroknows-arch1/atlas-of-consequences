# AOC-001 — Story Architecture v0.1

**Status:** Architecture complete; ready for factual/authenticity lock before prose drafting.

## Governing reader promise

AOC-001 begins with an ordinary AI interaction and progressively reveals the physical systems behind it, then narrows from the global copper system into one fictional Calama household.

The edition must never imply that AI caused existing Antofagasta mining investment, employment patterns, housing conditions, migration, water pressures, or Mauricio's opportunity.

**Mechanism:** AI/data-centre growth is an additional source of global electricity, grid and material pressure. Antofagasta already sits inside a large copper economy. Fiction explores what stronger or more persistent demand could mean for a household already living inside that economy.

**Constitution:** Facts can change the fiction. Fiction must never quietly become fact.

---

## Mobile-first reader journey

The master edition uses five clearly separated modes:

**STORY | WHAT'S REAL | CONSEQUENCES | PLACE | SOURCES**

The default journey is linear, but each factual hinge exposes a small route into the supporting mode without forcing the reader out of the story.

### 1. Opening — The question

**Reader experience**

Open on a globally ordinary act: a person types a question into an AI interface. Keep the question mundane enough that the reader recognises the behaviour. The answer appears quickly.

Then shift attention away from the words on the screen: the response did not come from nowhere.

Do not begin with statistics or a lecture. Use one concise physical reveal: machines, electricity, cooling, networks and buildings exist behind the apparently weightless interaction.

**Purpose**

Create the first reversal: AI feels digital; its operation is physical.

**Mode:** WHAT'S REAL.

**Evidence boundary**

Sourced claims may establish that data centres consume electricity and require physical infrastructure and cooling. Do not assign a precise energy, water or material footprint to the single fictional query unless a defensible methodology supports it. Do not imply every AI interaction has the same footprint.

**Reader exits**

- WHAT'S REAL → data centres, electricity and cooling.
- CONSEQUENCES → full chain preview.
- SOURCES → supporting evidence.

### 2. From computation to electricity and water

**Reader experience**

Pull back from the single interaction to expanding data-centre capacity. Show that scaling computation means scaling physical requirements: electricity supply, cooling and associated infrastructure.

Water appears here as a system requirement/context issue, not as a shortcut to a Chilean water-conflict story.

**Purpose**

Turn "the cloud" into buildings connected to power and cooling systems.

**Mode:** WHAT'S REAL.

**Evidence boundary**

Only make water claims at the geography and technology level supported by sources. Do not jump from data-centre cooling requirements to Calama household water pressure. No claim that AI caused Antofagasta water conditions.

**Reader exits**

- WHAT'S REAL → infrastructure explainer.
- CONSEQUENCES → AI/data centres → electricity/cooling.
- SOURCES.

### 3. Electricity becomes infrastructure

**Reader experience**

The camera moves outward again: electricity demand is not only generation. More load can require transmission, substations, transformers, cabling and other grid equipment.

Introduce the idea that digital growth can create demand for very non-digital things.

**Purpose**

Create the bridge from energy demand to materials without treating copper as the only material involved.

**Mode:** WHAT'S REAL.

**Evidence boundary**

Use sourced global/system-level evidence. Avoid claiming that a named grid project exists because of AI unless its project evidence explicitly establishes that link.

**Reader exits**

- CONSEQUENCES → electricity → grid expansion.
- WHAT'S REAL → grid/material explanation.
- SOURCES.

### 4. Copper enters

**Reader experience**

Move from wires and electrical equipment to copper's role in electrification, grids and data-centre infrastructure. The story's geography can now begin to turn toward where copper comes from.

A map/scroll transition can move from global infrastructure demand to Chile, then the Antofagasta Region.

**Purpose**

Make Chile a consequence of the material chain rather than an arbitrary story location.

**Mode:** WHAT'S REAL.

**Evidence boundary**

State copper's documented infrastructure role and Chile/Antofagasta production significance. AI is one participant in wider copper demand, not the sole or necessarily dominant explanation for regional mining activity. Do not claim AI caused a specific mine expansion, contract or job.

**Reader exits**

- CONSEQUENCES → grid → copper/material demand.
- PLACE → Chile / Antofagasta.
- SOURCES.

### 5. Antofagasta Region — the global chain meets an existing economy

**Reader experience**

Land in a region where copper already shapes work, investment, infrastructure and identity. Briefly establish that active mining investment, continuity projects, contracting and local-employment efforts exist independently of Atlas's AI thesis.

Then narrow: Antofagasta Region → El Loa → Calama → Chuquicamata relationship.

**Purpose**

Prevent the false causal leap "AI boom creates Calama mining economy." The reader sees an existing copper system receiving another possible source of global demand pressure.

**Mode:** WHAT'S REAL / PLACE.

**Evidence boundary**

Regional projects and labour initiatives must be described according to their actual documented causes/status. Historical and current conditions remain distinct. Mining is central but Calama is not reduced to mining. Housing, migration, crime, pollution, water or other social conditions do not enter the plot merely because research says they exist.

**Reader exits**

- PLACE → Calama / Chuquicamata mental map and everyday-life context.
- WHAT'S REAL → regional copper economy.
- SOURCES.

### 6. Calama — slow down before fiction

**Reader experience**

The scale changes. Replace global maps and infrastructure with ordinary city life: streets, school/work rhythms, neighbourhood activity, family logistics, recreation and the practical presence of mining rosters.

The reader should feel that Calama exists whether or not this Atlas edition exists.

**Purpose**

Establish a real place before introducing invented people.

**Mode:** PLACE.

**Evidence boundary**

Every generalised local condition must come from the Local Context Pack/everyday-life research and retain its proper scope. Do not manufacture slang, customs, attitudes, neighbourhood details or "typical" experiences. Mining dependence and mining support/criticism must not be flattened into a single community view.

**Reader exits**

- PLACE → deeper Calama context.
- SOURCES.

---

## 7. The fact/fiction handover

Use a hard visual break, not a subtle prose transition.

**Label: STORY — FICTION**

Suggested boundary copy:

> Daniela, Mauricio, Tomás and Emilia are fictional. Their individual lives and the events that follow are invented. The place, wider mining economy and documented work patterns surrounding them are evidence-informed. Mauricio's opportunity is fictional and is not presented as a job created by AI.

The interface should retain a persistent STORY marker while the narrative is active.

This is the point where factual reporting stops determining events. Evidence continues to constrain the world in which those events may plausibly occur.

---

## 8. Daniela enters — household continuity

**Narrative function**

Enter through Daniela, not Mauricio and not a mine site.

Begin with an ordinary task during a Mauricio-away period. The exact task is chosen later after authenticity checks: school logistics, work, groceries, a household repair, family messaging, or another researched-plausible routine.

Show that Daniela has a functioning life and work of her own. She is competent because this is her household, not because the plot needs a long-suffering mining partner.

Mauricio is present indirectly through messages/calls and the household's expectations of when he returns.

**Emotional turn 1:** the household works without him.

**Evidence boundary**

Daniela's exact occupation, neighbourhood, school routines, transport and domestic details remain provisional until verified. Her thoughts, actions and relationships are fiction. Do not present her experience as representative of Calama families generally.

**Reader exits**

- PLACE → roster/commuting context.
- WHAT'S REAL → evidence on mining shift/commuting family effects.

## 9. Mauricio returns — affection plus readjustment

**Narrative function**

His return is welcome and ordinary, not cinematic.

Use behaviour to show the double rhythm:
- he tries to help with something whose routine changed while he was away;
- Emilia instinctively goes to Daniela for something even though Mauricio is present;
- Tomás has a small piece of life Mauricio needs to catch up on;
- Daniela must make room for another decision-maker inside a system she has been running.

Include humour or warmth so absence is not translated into emotional failure.

**Emotional turn 2:** being home does not instantly restore the time missed.

**Evidence boundary**

These incidents are invented. They are permitted by evidence that roster/commuting arrangements can affect family and parenting life, but Atlas must not claim these exact behaviours are typical or statistically prevalent.

## 10. Mauricio's opportunity

**Narrative function**

Introduce the first story pressure as genuinely attractive.

Mauricio receives or becomes eligible for a meaningful step forward in mining-related contracting/technical work. It may offer better pay, duration, training, responsibility or career security.

Do not yet lock employer, mine, salary, roster or programme.

Mauricio sees provision: more stability, a future plan, perhaps more room for the family to choose later.

Daniela sees the same benefit and immediately understands the time cost.

Neither is wrong.

**Emotional turn 3:** good news creates a harder question.

**Evidence boundary**

The opportunity is explicitly fictional. Existing regional investment, contracting and labour demand establish plausibility only. No wording may imply a documented chain AI → regional project → Mauricio's job.

**Reader exits**

- WHAT'S REAL → active regional mining/contracting context.
- CONSEQUENCES → opportunity-not-causation explanation.
- SOURCES.

## 11. The household calculates the opportunity

**Narrative function**

Do not turn this into an exposition scene. Let the calculation happen through ordinary plans.

Possible story objects, to be selected only after economic/authenticity checks:
- a savings target;
- an expense they could absorb more easily;
- education/training choices;
- a modest family plan delayed by uncertainty;
- calendar/roster implications.

Daniela and Mauricio should both be able to articulate what the opportunity gives them and what it asks.

**Emotional turn 4:** money can solve real problems without solving time.

**Evidence boundary**

Any salary, expense, schooling, housing or consumer detail must be checked against plausible local economics before prose drafting. Avoid "high mining wages = wealthy household."

## 12. Tomás — the opportunity becomes a future

**Narrative function**

Tomás sees Mauricio's step forward as evidence that mining-linked technical work can be a serious path. He also sees its cost because he lives with the roster.

His role is not to announce whether mining is good or bad. Give him a concrete near-future decision appropriate to a 16-year-old once Chilean education/training pathways are verified.

Parents may read his future differently:
- Mauricio can see a route to security he understands.
- Daniela can want Tomás to have genuine choice rather than inherit necessity.

Tomás remains undecided.

**Emotional turn 5:** Mauricio's opportunity changes what "a sensible future" looks like inside the family.

**Evidence boundary**

Exact educational pathways and career routes require verification. Do not claim regional youth generally choose mining for these reasons. Tomás's preferences are fictional.

**Reader exits**

- PLACE / WHAT'S REAL → verified local education/training context once completed.
- CONSEQUENCES → intergenerational pathway as a possible consequence, not a predicted outcome.

## 13. Emilia — the cost is shown at child scale

**Narrative function**

Emilia remains seven. Give her a small concern that matters completely to her and barely registers against the global chain.

Use one quiet behaviour, not a speech, to reveal the household adaptation to absence. The strongest current candidate is that she automatically asks Daniela for help while Mauricio is available, followed later by a warm Mauricio/Emilia moment that proves their bond is intact.

Do not use the real anecdote behind the design principle.

**Emotional turn 6:** Mauricio can be a loving father and still be absent from pieces of his children's lives.

**Evidence boundary**

The incident is invented. It illustrates one plausible consequence; it is not evidence about mining families as a group.

## 14. Decision / departure

**Narrative function**

The first edition does not need a catastrophic climax. The family chooses, provisionally, to take the opportunity or move toward it because the benefits are real.

The emotional climax is the family adjusting to what they have chosen.

Before the next departure, avoid a dramatic farewell. Normal preparations carry more weight: bag, messages, calendar, a forgotten detail, someone asking who will handle something while he is away.

Daniela's position should not resolve into martyrdom or opposition. Mauricio's should not resolve into "career over family." They are making a rational family decision under imperfect conditions.

**Emotional turn 7:** choosing the opportunity does not erase its price.

**Evidence boundary**

All events are fiction. The story is exploring a plausible consequence, not forecasting what stronger copper demand will do to Calama households.

## 15. Ending — return to the question

End small, then widen once.

Recommended shape: after Mauricio leaves, the household resumes its away-rhythm. Daniela handles an ordinary task; Tomás continues whatever future-facing decision has been established; Emilia does something unmistakably childlike.

Then briefly reconnect the reader to the opening AI interaction—not by having the family discuss AI, but by reminding the reader that distant digital demand and this household can occupy the same physical system without ever meeting or knowing one another.

Final question, expressed rather than answered:

**What does providing for your family mean when the opportunity that helps you provide also asks for more of your absence?**

The ending must preserve uncertainty. Atlas has shown a possible human consequence, not a proven individual causal chain.

**Reader exits**

- WHAT'S REAL → what is documented versus invented.
- CONSEQUENCES → full chain with confidence/causal boundaries.
- PLACE → Calama, Chuquicamata and regional context.
- SOURCES → complete evidence register.

## Consequence chain displayed after STORY

**Documented global mechanism**
AI/data-centre expansion
→ additional electricity/cooling requirements
→ pressure for power/grid/infrastructure expansion
→ material requirements including copper.

**Documented place connection**
Chile / Antofagasta Region
→ major existing copper economy
→ active mining investment, continuity, contracting and employment activity.

**Fictional exploration**
Mauricio's invented career opportunity
→ improved household financial outlook
→ harder roster/home balance
→ Daniela absorbs/negotiates household continuity
→ Tomás sees a stronger mining-linked option for his future
→ Emilia reveals absence through ordinary behaviour.

**Forbidden shortcut**
AI → caused Mauricio's job / caused existing Antofagasta projects / caused existing local social conditions.

## Evidence gates before prose

The architecture is complete, but finished STORY prose should not lock the following until checked:

1. Mauricio's exact skilled/technical occupation and credible employer type.
2. A plausible roster/change in roster associated with the fictional step forward.
3. Daniela's exact occupation and work rhythm.
4. Household economics sufficient to make the opportunity materially meaningful without caricature.
5. Tomás's realistic age-appropriate Chilean education/training choices.
6. Emilia's school/family routine details.
7. Neighbourhood/geography and travel times used in scenes.
8. Contemporary Chilean/Calameño dialogue and terminology.
9. Any extended-family biography, especially Chuquicamata relocation memory, against chronology.
10. Any serious social condition introduced later must pass its own place/population/period evidence gate.

---

## Lean edition shape

Keep v0.1 to roughly **8–10 narrative movements** across the factual opening and fictional household. Do not add secondary crises merely to create plot.

The edition succeeds if the reader can:
1. feel the physicality behind an AI interaction;
2. follow the chain to copper and Calama;
3. know exactly where fact ends and fiction begins;
4. care about Daniela's household independently of the thesis;
5. understand Mauricio's opportunity as both beneficial and costly;
6. leave knowing this is a plausible consequence exploration, not a claim that AI caused this family's life.
