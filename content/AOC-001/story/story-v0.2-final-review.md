## Narrative review

Daniela remains the primary household anchor without being reduced to Mauricio's partner or household administrator.

The opportunity remains genuinely attractive. The story does not manufacture a moral failure to create tension.

### Tomás — PASS

Tomás remains undecided.

### Emilia — PASS

### Elena — PASS

Elena now functions as family rather than as a delivery mechanism for historical research.

Removing the Chuquicamata relocation exposition was the correct editorial decision. Her researched chronology remains available for later use but does not need to appear in AOC-001.

## Evidence review

### Fact / fiction boundary — PASS

The opening disclosure explicitly identifies Daniela, Mauricio, Tomás, Emilia and Elena as fictional and states that Mauricio's opportunity is fictional.

The story does not present character events as testimony or documented experience.

### Causation boundary — PASS

The story does **not** claim:
- AI created Mauricio's role;
- a data centre caused his promotion;
- AI caused a particular Antofagasta mining project;
- this household is representative of Calama families generally.

The ending now makes only the broader proposition that digital systems require a physical world. The detailed causal chain belongs in WHAT'S REAL and CONSEQUENCES.

### Work / roster boundary — PASS

The story uses:
- 7x7;
- rotating work;
- night-work recovery;
- organised movement toward the faena.

It does not invent exact shift hours, precise rotation sequences, named employers, named mines, technical procedures or commute times.

### Economic boundary — PASS

The story uses better pay and savings/future optionality without assigning invented salaries, property status, debt levels or precise household expenses.

### Education boundary — PASS

Tomás's technical interest remains broad. No unsupported school, programme or guaranteed career pathway is introduced.

### Geography boundary — PASS

Calama is home and the faena is a separate work environment. The story avoids invented neighbourhoods, pickup points and exact travel times.

### Language boundary — PASS

The English dialogue remains translation-transparent. *Faena* is used sparingly and meaningfully. No decorative Chilean slang or invented technical jargon has been introduced.

### Serious-condition gate — PASS / NOT TRIGGERED

The story does not introduce crime, abuse, addiction, discrimination, housing hardship, water hardship, pollution illness or another serious local condition requiring a new evidence gate.
