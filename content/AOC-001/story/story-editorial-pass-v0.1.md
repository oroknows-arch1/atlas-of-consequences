# AOC-001 — STORY Editorial Pass v0.1

**Status:** First editorial pass complete  
**Source reviewed:** STORY v0.1  
**Purpose:** Test pacing, emotional realism, character voice, over-explanation and thesis intrusion before revision.

## Verdict

**The story works. Revise; do not rebuild.**

Its strongest material is the ordinary household behaviour: the missing lid, toast, the paper-cup message, Emilia asking Daniela for help while Mauricio is beside her, Tomás asking “What roster?”, the broken drawer exchange, and Mauricio failing Emilia's listening test.

Those moments make the family believable without requiring a secondary crisis.

The main weakness is that the narrator occasionally explains the meaning immediately after the scene has already shown it. The next revision should trust the reader more.

## Keep

### Opening household rhythm

Keep the Tuesday opening substantially intact. It establishes Daniela as a working parent with an independent life before the mining/AI thesis reaches the household.

Especially keep:
- one shoe / container lid;
- Tomás and the toast;
- Mauricio's mundane paper-cup message;
- “Home Thursday.”

These are more valuable than additional Calama exposition.

### Emilia's Daniela-first moment

Keep almost exactly as designed.

Mauricio's “I am literally here,” Emilia's “Oh,” and his “Mostly” smile are the cleanest expression of the story's central cost.

Do not add explanation to this scene.

### The offer conversation

Keep:
- “Seven-seven.”
- “Rotating.”
- “I know how numbers work, Mauricio.”
- “What happens after nights?” / “I sleep.” / “Exactly.”

This gives the roster consequence through the couple rather than through exposition.

### Tomás

Keep “What roster?” as the centre of his scene.

His immediate understanding does more work than a speech about mining families would.

### Broken drawer exchange

Keep. It gives Mauricio useful domestic presence and prevents the story reducing him to absence.

### Emilia's listening test

Keep. It provides warmth immediately before the departure sequence and shows Mauricio actively trying to be present.

## Tighten

### “That was the point.”

Current:
> Nothing about the morning was unusual. That was the point.

The first sentence can stay. The second is unnecessary. The scene has already established ordinariness.

### “Mauricio was part of the system.”

This is thematically accurate but sounds like Atlas architecture entering the narrator.

Prefer concrete household language or remove it. The surrounding paragraph already shows Daniela knowing how the home works while he is away.

### Financial explanation after the offer

The section beginning:
> Still, the money mattered.

contains useful material, but it explains the economic lock too completely.

Retain the distinction that they are stable rather than desperate and that the extra income means more choice. Compress the surrounding explanation.

The later calendar conversation already makes the “buffer = ability to choose” idea much more naturally.

### Tomás pathway explanation

Current narration explains:
> There were other possibilities... technical pathway... broader...

This reads partly like the education research note.

Let the browser tabs/dialogue imply that he is comparing routes. Keep only enough narration to establish that Mauricio sees a practical path but does not want to choose it for him.

### Elena section

Elena is useful, but the current passage:
> She had lived long enough around mining...
> Years earlier...
> She almost mentioned it...

comes close to inserting the chronology lock because it exists.

The strongest Elena material is her personality: coffee, direct questioning, Mauricio hiding behind his phone.

Revision rule: either give her Chuquicamata memory a concrete natural trigger or leave the history out of STORY v0.2. Do not retain a historical reference merely to prove the research was done.

### Sunday explanatory sequence

The repeated “It appeared...” structure works, but five examples are one too many. Cut one or two.

### “That was home too.”

The broken-drawer messages already demonstrate this. Compress the following explanatory lines.

### “They were leverage against their own future.”

Good concept, slightly authorial phrasing. Daniela's own line—“For being able to choose”—is stronger. Trust it.

## Remove or relocate

### Final global explanation

The final section currently does too much factual boundary work inside STORY:

> Behind it were buildings, electricity, cooling systems, grids, cables, metals, mines and people.
>
> No straight line ran...
>
> No single query...
>
> No data centre...

The causal safeguards are correct, but several belong in WHAT'S REAL / CONSEQUENCES rather than in the emotional ending.

STORY should not finish by sounding like an evidence disclaimer.

Revision target:
- keep one brief return to the apparently weightless AI interaction;
- keep the physical-world idea;
- remove most explicit causal-defence sentences from the fictional ending;
- let the persistent STORY/FICTION label and adjacent CONSEQUENCES material carry the formal boundary;
- land quickly on the central question.

The ending should widen once, not reopen the report.

## Voice notes

### Daniela

Strongest voice in v0.1.

She is practical without becoming cold, financially aware without being written as the household accountant, and supportive without being passive.

Preserve her dry humour.

### Mauricio

Works best when he is lightly defensive, useful, affectionate and occasionally behind the household rhythm.

Avoid making him too philosophically articulate about provision. His actions should carry more of that theme.

### Tomás

Credible when brief.

Do not expand him into a spokesperson for youth/mining choices. His “What roster?” moment is enough to establish that he already understands part of the cost.

### Emilia

Strong and age-scaled.

Continue avoiding thematic dialogue.

### Elena

Personality is present immediately. History is not yet organically integrated.

She can survive the revision even if the Chuquicamata reference is removed.

## Pacing

The first half moves well.

The middle slows because three explanatory layers accumulate:
1. financial interpretation;
2. Tomás pathway interpretation;
3. Elena historical interpretation.

Revision should remove explanation rather than remove characters.

The final third recovers through the drawer messages, calendar conversation, Emilia scene and departure.

## Emotional realism

**PASS.**

The family is not presented as broken.

Mauricio is not a bad father.
Daniela is not a martyr.
The children are not traumatised plot devices.
The opportunity is not secretly a disaster.
The family can rationally choose it while still paying a cost.

Preserve this ambiguity.

## Thesis intrusion

**MODERATE — needs reduction.**

The thesis is strongest when invisible inside the household.

The current draft sometimes tells the reader what a moment means immediately after showing it. Revision should follow this rule:

**If the behaviour already carries the consequence, delete the explanation.**

## Revision target

STORY v0.2 should be modestly shorter, not dramatically rewritten.

Target changes:
- remove repeated interpretation;
- make Tomás's education choice less explanatory;
- make Elena's history organic or omit it;
- let Daniela's “For being able to choose” carry the financial theme;
- shorten the global ending;
- preserve humour and mundane household detail;
- preserve all fact/fiction and causation safeguards at the publishing-package level.

## Next single action

Create **AOC-001 STORY v0.2** from this editorial pass.

Do not add plot. Do not add research. Do not make the family more dramatic. The revision is primarily subtraction: trust the scenes, tighten the narrator and let the reader make more of the connections.
