# AOC-001 — Household Economics Authenticity Lock v0.1

**Status:** Evidence-supported fictional design lock.  
**Scope:** Household economic position and the meaning of Mauricio's opportunity.  
**Date:** 2026-09-22.

## Locked household position

Daniela and Mauricio are a **two-income, economically stable working household**.

They are not poor, secretly destitute, affluent, or insulated from ordinary financial decisions. Mauricio's mining-maintenance work provides the stronger income; Daniela's administrative work is a meaningful independent contribution.

Atlas should portray them as able to meet normal household costs and absorb some discretionary spending, while still having reasons to care about savings, education/training costs, repairs, travel, children's activities and future security.

## Evidence anchor

The latest official INE income survey available for the region reports that in 2025:
- Antofagasta Region's mean net monthly labour income was CLP 1,196,754;
- its median was CLP 830,350;
- private-sector employees had mean net monthly income of CLP 1,288,153 and median income of CLP 897,664;
- men's regional mean/median were CLP 1,426,315 / 950,308;
- women's regional mean/median were CLP 879,320 / 700,000.

These are regional individual-worker figures, not Calama household budgets and not salaries for Mauricio or Daniela.

A 2026 Calama technical-maintenance vacancy published through Chile's Bolsa Nacional de Empleo advertised CLP 1,060,001–1,210,000 for a technician with relevant industrial/mining-equipment experience. This is useful as an occupation-level plausibility anchor, not as Mauricio's salary.

A contemporary Calama administrative vacancy confirms local full-time administrative employment but reserves the salary, so Atlas should not manufacture a precise market wage for Daniela from that listing.

## Salary treatment in the story

**Do not give either character an exact salary in v0.1 unless a scene genuinely requires it.**

The evidence is strong enough to establish the relationship:
- Mauricio earns the larger share of household employment income;
- Daniela's earnings materially support the household and her independence;
- the household has more resilience than a single-income family would;
- losing either income would matter.

This is more defensible than inventing a precise combined monthly budget.

## Locked meaning of Mauricio's opportunity

The step-up role provides a **noticeable but not transformative improvement** in household finances.

The increase is large enough for Daniela and Mauricio to discuss what it could accelerate, but not large enough to make ordinary constraints disappear.

For story purposes, the opportunity should affect **one concrete medium-term family objective**, rather than a shopping list of benefits.

Best current story object:

**a household savings buffer that gives the family more freedom over future decisions.**

That can later be expressed through something specific—education/training, a home-related expense, travel to family, or another verified need—but the first edition should avoid turning Mauricio's opportunity into consumer reward.

The deeper benefit is optionality: a little more money saved can make the family less dependent on always accepting the next roster purely because it pays better.

## What the family can plausibly look like

Permitted:
- ordinary technology and children's activities;
- occasional meals/outings;
- dealing with an unexpected expense without immediate crisis;
- saving toward a future goal;
- discussing whether an expense is worth it;
- Daniela's income being economically consequential;
- Mauricio occasionally using spending as a way of enjoying limited home time, if handled subtly.

Avoid:
- portraying them as impoverished despite two stable jobs solely to create stakes;
- equating mining employment with automatic wealth;
- luxury purchases as shorthand for mining income;
- precise debt, rent, mortgage or school-fee burdens until separately evidenced;
- assuming they own their home;
- treating regional average income as their personal income.

## Story consequence

The opportunity now has a clear trade:

**Mauricio's step-up role can improve the family's financial margin and future freedom, while the rotating roster can reduce the amount and predictability of usable shared family time.**

This makes both positions rational.

Mauricio can think: *this helps us get ahead.*

Daniela can think: *getting ahead should eventually give us more control over our lives, not permanently less.*

Those are fictional viewpoints, not statements about mining households generally.

## Evidence boundary

### Supported reality

Antofagasta has relatively high labour incomes compared with Chile overall, while its income distribution remains broad. Contemporary Calama evidence supports paid technical maintenance and administrative employment.

### Fiction

Mauricio's income, Daniela's income, their combined finances, expenses, savings, financial ambitions and the value of Mauricio's fictional offer are invented.

### Prohibited inference

Do not calculate the fictional household's finances by simply adding regional averages. Do not claim their economic position is typical of Calama or of mining households.

## Details intentionally not locked

- exact monthly salaries;
- percentage pay increase;
- rent/mortgage/home ownership;
- debt;
- savings balance;
- vehicle ownership;
- school costs;
- precise household expenditure;
- final concrete savings goal.

Lock these only if a later scene requires them and supporting evidence can bound them credibly.
