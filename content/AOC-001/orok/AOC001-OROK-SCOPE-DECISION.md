# AOC-001 — OROK Companion Scope Decision

**Edition:** AOC-001 — *AI's Physical Hunger*  
**Decision date:** 2026-09-25  
**Authority:** human approval  
**Status:** APPROVED

## Decision

An OROK companion is **not required** for AOC-001 release and is **not required** to prove the Atlas end-to-end publishing pipeline.

OROK remains available as an optional downstream educational or cultural companion when an edition has a useful, clearly scoped reason for one.

## Release effect

- AOC-001 may proceed through publication QA and release approval without producing an OROK companion.
- The absence of an OROK companion is not a publication blocker.
- No OROK content needs to be created merely to satisfy the AOC-001 prototype test.
- If an OROK companion is produced later, it is a separate derivative with its own editorial scope and evidence/cultural boundaries.
- Atlas factual, fictional, evidence and distribution requirements remain unchanged.

## General rule established by this test

OROK companion material is **optional downstream enrichment**, not a mandatory node in the Atlas edition release path unless a future edition explicitly opts into it before production.

A future edition may elect to include an OROK companion, but that election must be explicit; absence alone must not silently become a release failure.
