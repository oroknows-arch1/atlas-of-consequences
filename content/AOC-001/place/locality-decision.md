# AOC-001 — Locality Decision

## Selected locality
**Antofagasta Region, Chile**

Status: **Selected for v0.1 research and story development.**

The human narrative may later be narrowed to a specific city, town, household, workplace, or corridor inside the region after the Local Context Pack is deep enough. That is a story-development decision, not a reason to widen the v0.1 ecosystem.

## Why Antofagasta

Antofagasta gives AOC-001 a strong evidence bridge between the abstract growth of AI infrastructure and a real place whose economy and communities are deeply connected to copper.

The evidence currently supports these separate facts:

1. Chile is deliberately expanding its data-centre sector. Chile's National Data Center Plan reports capacity growing from 35 MW in 2013 to 198 MW in 2023 and projects capacity could triple over the following five years.
2. AI/data-centre infrastructure has substantial physical-material requirements. A 2026 peer-reviewed modelling study found copper represented 83% of the modelled mineral mass for AI data-centre infrastructure, with grid transmission and distribution responsible for most of the modelled copper demand.
3. Antofagasta is Chile's principal copper-producing region. Cochilco projects it at roughly half of national copper production.
4. Chile's 2025–2034 mining investment portfolio places Antofagasta first among regions, with roughly US$40.2 billion in projected mining investment, heavily associated with copper expansion and operational continuity.
5. The human layer is already measurable. Regional research documents unusually high mining labour commuting, while INE data records high labour incomes alongside unemployment and other labour-market conditions. University research identifies housing/service costs and social/environmental conditions as factors discouraging mining workers from residing permanently in Antofagasta and Calama.
6. Water is not an abstract issue in the region. Current infrastructure includes major desalination proposals for human and industrial consumption.

## Critical fact boundary

**Atlas must NOT say that AI demand caused Antofagasta's current mining investment, employment patterns, housing conditions, migration, or water pressures unless direct evidence establishes that causal link.**

The defensible chain at this stage is:

AI/data-centre expansion
→ documented additional demand for electricity/grid infrastructure and copper
→ Antofagasta is a globally significant copper-producing locality with major existing/planned investment
→ therefore changing global copper demand is a legitimate pressure to investigate here
→ local employment, commuting, housing, water, family, and community consequences must each be independently researched before being represented as factual outcomes.

Where evidence stops, Atlas may explore plausible consequences only inside clearly labelled fiction.

## Why this works for the first Atlas edition

Antofagasta lets the reader travel from an ordinary digital action to physical infrastructure and then to a human place without pretending the entire consequence chain is already proven.

It also provides strong Spanish-language government, statistical, university, industry, and local-media source ecosystems for the Local Context Pack.

## Next research gate

Before drafting STORY, build the Antofagasta Local Context Pack around:

- copper operations and investment
- electricity and water systems
- mining workforce and labour commuting
- housing and cost of living
- migration and population
- family/community life
- Indigenous/local histories and communities where relevant
- environmental debates
- local terminology and everyday geography
- local reporting and locally expressed views

Only after this research should Atlas choose the fictional character, household, occupation, and exact setting.
