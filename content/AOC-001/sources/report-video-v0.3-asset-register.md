## Family-image boundary

The v0.3 source search did **not** identify a clean, rights-cleared contemporary photograph of a specific Calama/Chuquicamata miner with their family that could be used without implying unsupported personal linkage.

Therefore v0.3 must **not** substitute a generic stock family or an unrelated Chilean family. The factual montage may show:
- real Chilean miners, explicitly labelled to their true context; and
- real Calama homes/community context, explicitly labelled as community context.

A literal miner-family image should only be added later if a properly licensed, region-specific source with clear context is found.

## Editorial causal boundary

This montage connects:

`Anthropic / Western Downs data-centre story → physical electricity/material systems → copper → existing northern Chile mining economy → workers / homes / community`

It does **not** establish:
- that Anthropic buys copper from Chuquicamata;
- that the Western Downs project causes a specific Chilean mine expansion;
- that any photographed miner or household is personally affected by Anthropic;
- that the fictional Atlas family is a real photographed household.

**Connection is allowed. Causation requires evidence.**
