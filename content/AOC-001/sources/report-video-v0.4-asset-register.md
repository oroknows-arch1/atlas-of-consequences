## Editorial causal boundary

The montage connects:

`Anthropic / San Francisco → Reuters Western Downs agreement → physical data-centre and grid context → copper/material systems → existing northern-Chile copper economy → miners → Calama community context`

It does **not** establish:
- that Anthropic buys copper from Chuquicamata;
- that the Western Downs project causes a specific Chilean mine expansion;
- that any photographed miner or household is personally affected by Anthropic;
- that the fictional Atlas family is a real photographed household.

**Connection is allowed. Causation requires evidence.**
