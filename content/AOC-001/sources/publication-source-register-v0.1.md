# AOC-001 — Publication Source Register v0.1

**Status:** Publication evidence register complete  
**Edition:** AOC-001 — *AI's Physical Hunger*  
**Purpose:** Make every material factual claim in Master Edition v0.1 inspectable without cluttering STORY.

## Evidence rule

This register supports factual statements in WHAT'S REAL, CONSEQUENCES and PLACE. It does **not** turn Daniela, Mauricio, Tomás, Emilia, Elena or their events into factual people or testimony.

A source proves only the claim assigned to it. Evidence for AI electricity demand does not prove a local employment consequence in Calama.

## SR-01 — Global data-centre electricity use

**Claim in edition:** Data centres consumed about 415 TWh in 2024, around 1.5% of global electricity consumption.

**Source:** *Energy and AI — Executive summary*  
**Publisher:** International Energy Agency (IEA)  
**Published:** 10 April 2025  
**Direct URL:** https://www.iea.org/reports/energy-and-ai/executive-summary  
**Supports:** 415 TWh and approximately 1.5% global electricity share in 2024.  
**Limitation:** Data-centre total; it is not the electricity use of AI alone and cannot be converted into one universal per-query footprint.

## SR-02 — AI and projected data-centre electricity growth

**Source:** *Energy and AI — Energy demand from AI*  
**Publisher:** IEA  
**Published:** 2025  
**Direct URL:** https://www.iea.org/reports/energy-and-ai/energy-demand-from-ai

# Source hierarchy

For publication, prefer:
1. official statistics / regulator / intergovernmental analysis;
2. academic research;
3. operational primary sources for facts about the organisation's own operations;
4. local reporting and testimony for lived context, clearly attributed.

Do not use a company source as independent proof that its own project produced positive community outcomes.
