# AOC-002 — research and new-footage sourcing pass 01 (2026-11-24)

**Production subject:** hypothetical drilling-pad failure, Eastern Niger Delta. This document does not incorporate Aku-01 or any AOC-004 assets.

## Verified environmental context
- Nigeria's second biennial UNFCCC report describes the Niger Delta as low-lying, crossed by channels, with freshwater, mangrove-swamp and coastal-sand/beach-ridge zones. https://unfccc.int/sites/default/files/resource/NIGERIA%2520BUR%25202%2520-%2520Second%2520Biennial%2520Update%2520Report%2520%2528BUR2%2529.pdf
- IUCN 2024 report distinguishes lowland forests, upper freshwater floodplain, lower tidal floodplain with mangroves/estuaries/creeks, and barrier islands. These are *regional alternatives*, not evidence that the hypothetical rig occupied any one habitat. https://portals.iucn.org/library/efiles/documents/2024-014-En.pdf
- API RP 4G (2019), section 15.1, says foundations must account for local ground bearing capacity, concentrated rig loads, distribution of those loads and site preparation/drainage. Cite the standard as an engineering principle, not a diagnosis of this incident. https://www.ipgmservicios.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/API-RP-4G-2019-Operation-Inspection-Maintenance-and-Repair-of-Drilling-and-Well-Servicing-Subestructures.pdf
