# AOC-002 — Contractor liaison and translator | interactive scene draft

**Fictional composite perspective.** Not documentary testimony. No named real operator, contractor, community or actual incident site.

## Entry
Different teams may use different technical terms and reporting lines. The fictional liaison must make a safety concern understandable without assigning blame to a nationality.

**Prompt:** What would you do next?

### Choice A — Translate and confirm the concern
Each party repeats the same operational question in its own words before decisions proceed.

### Choice B — Create one signed action register
Open questions, owners and deadlines become visible, but signatures do not validate engineering assumptions.

### Choice C — Escalate directly to decision makers
The issue reaches authorised personnel sooner, while the site remains subject to safety controls.

**Perspective handoff:** rig crew member.

## Editorial gates
All outcomes are conditional possibilities, not proven historical events. Never imply a real Chinese drilling contractor, Nigerian civil contractor or oil operator was negligent. Keep fact/fiction labels visible and offer a return to the six-perspective chooser. Source register must distinguish client-supplied claims from independently verified engineering or geographic facts.
