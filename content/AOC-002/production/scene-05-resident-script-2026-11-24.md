# AOC-002 — Nearby resident | interactive scene draft

**Fictional composite perspective.** Not documentary testimony. No named real operator, contractor, community or actual incident site.

## Entry
A resident hears conflicting accounts about an industrial site. No pollution, injury or displacement has been verified.

**Prompt:** What would you do next?

### Choice A — Request a community briefing
The resident asks what happened, what remains unknown and how safety information will be shared.

### Choice B — Document local observations
Dates, photos and observations may help distinguish evidence from rumours without proving impacts.

### Choice C — Organise neighbours' questions
Collective questions can focus on access, traffic and water safeguards without alleging contamination.

**Perspective handoff:** civil supervisor.

## Editorial gates
All outcomes are conditional possibilities, not proven historical events. Never imply a real Chinese drilling contractor, Nigerian civil contractor or oil operator was negligent. Keep fact/fiction labels visible and offer a return to the six-perspective chooser. Source register must distinguish client-supplied claims from independently verified engineering or geographic facts.
