# AOC-002 — Rig crew member | interactive scene draft

**Fictional composite perspective.** Not documentary testimony. No named real operator, contractor, community or actual incident site.

## Entry
A vibration, a shift, an observation: none proves a foundation failure. The crew member must decide whether to stop and report, ask for a second assessment, or continue under existing instructions.

**Prompt:** What would you do next?

### Choice A — Stop and report
Operations pause while qualified staff assess the observation; uncertainty is made visible.

### Choice B — Request an independent check
Another set of eyes may clarify the observation, but only authorised personnel can decide restart.

### Choice C — Continue under current instructions
Work continues, but an unresolved concern remains; this does not establish an accident.

**Perspective handoff:** wellsite engineer.

## Editorial gates
All outcomes are conditional possibilities, not proven historical events. Never imply a real Chinese drilling contractor, Nigerian civil contractor or oil operator was negligent. Keep fact/fiction labels visible and offer a return to the six-perspective chooser.
