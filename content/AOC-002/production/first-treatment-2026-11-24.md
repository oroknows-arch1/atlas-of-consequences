## Editorial premise
A drilling rig stands on a platform built to carry enormous loads. What happens if the ground beneath that platform cannot support them? A fictionalised, multi-perspective exploration prompted by an unverified client account of a pad failure in the Eastern Niger Delta. No named company or actual site is accused.

## Opening film — AOC-001-style, phone-first, no soundtrack
1. **REAL CONTEXT (0–8s):** authentic rights-cleared footage of waterways, wet terrain or onshore industrial activity in the broader Niger Delta, geographically verified and labelled as *regional context, not the incident*. No generic overseas rig footage.
2. **THE QUESTION (8–16s):** quiet, restrained caption over verified real contextual footage: “A drilling rig depends on the ground beneath it.” The specific engineering statement needs an independent technical source before final release.
3. **SCENARIO (16–26s):** title card and explicit label “Hypothetical reconstruction based on an unverified account.” Caption: “A drilling pad gives way. A rig settles. Work stops.” This is storytelling, not incident footage.
4. **HANDOFF (26–35s):** deliberate cut into the first fictional viewpoint. Label remains clearly visible. Skip-introduction control; no autoplay replay or double trigger. Timings are provisional editorial targets, not produced assets.

## Fictional perspective outlines (all composite characters, no real persons implied)
- **Civil site supervisor:** asked to hand over a platform; tension between schedule, soil test results, documentation and who accepts the work. No claim that a test was omitted.
- **Drilling crew member:** sees warning signs and weighs stop-work authority against pressure to continue. No invented injury or fatality.
- **Operator's wellsite engineer:** must decide what evidence supports continued operations and how to communicate uncertainty.
- **Local logistics provider:** equipment stoppage may disrupt contracted work and household income; no claim that it actually did.
- **Nearby resident:** asks how access, water, noise and safety information are managed; no assertion of contamination or displacement.
- **Contractor liaison/translator:** explores handover and language across Nigerian and Chinese teams without caricature or invented misconduct.
