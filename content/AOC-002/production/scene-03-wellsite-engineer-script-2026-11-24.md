# AOC-002 — Wellsite engineer | interactive scene draft

**Fictional composite perspective.** Not documentary testimony. No named real operator, contractor, community or actual incident site.

## Entry
After the reported settlement, the engineer must decide what is needed before any proposed restart. The fictional incident does not establish the actual technical cause.

**Prompt:** What would you do next?

### Choice A — Commission a ground-and-load review
Restart depends on evidence about bearing capacity, load paths and the condition of the site.

### Choice B — Prioritise securing the area
The immediate focus is personnel safety and preventing further exposure while evidence is gathered.

### Choice C — Ask contractors for a joint incident record
Different records are reconciled; agreement about facts is not a substitute for engineering verification.

**Perspective handoff:** contractor liaison.
