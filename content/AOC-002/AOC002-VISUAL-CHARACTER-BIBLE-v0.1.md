## Story world

The fiction stays with one Owerri household and Chinedu's small repair workshop. Owerri is a supported regional urban setting, not the Aku-01 wellhead or an identified host community. The visual mechanism is ordinary anticipation: a discovery announcement makes rumour and possibility move before production, jobs or community benefits exist.

## Adaeze

**Role:** Electrical-engineering graduate living in Owerri; Nneka's daughter; Chinedu's niece.  
**Established appearance:** Young adult Nigerian woman; dark skin; braided hair pulled back; practical short-sleeved clothing; focused, reserved expression.  
**Established behaviour:** Reading and verifying information; working hands-on with electrical equipment; precise rather than performative.  
**Continuity rule:** Household and workshop images must remain recognisably the same woman. Do not turn her into an oil-company employee, field engineer, witness or host-community representative.

## Nneka

**Role:** Adaeze's mother and a market trader.  
**Established appearance:** Older Nigerian woman; dark skin; patterned dress and headwrap; composed, practical bearing.  
**Established behaviour:** Household accounting and food/market preparation.  
**Continuity rule:** Her work and questions express household calculation, not poverty spectacle or a claim about a typical Imo household.

## Chinedu

**Role:** Adaeze's uncle; runs a small vehicle-repair workshop.  
**Established appearance:** Older Nigerian man; dark skin; close-cropped hair; short-sleeved checked work shirt.  
**Established behaviour:** Repairing motors and sharing workshop space with Adaeze.  
**Continuity rule:** Do not add branded uniforms, oil-field PPE or operator affiliation. His workshop is fictional and is not a contractor to Aku-01.
