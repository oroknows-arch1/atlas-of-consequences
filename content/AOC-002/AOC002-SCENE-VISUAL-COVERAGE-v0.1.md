### Generation lock

- Match Adaeze, Nneka, Chinedu, the household and workshop to the two existing anchors.
- Photorealistic cinematic fiction; visible `STORY / FICTION` treatment in the assembled reader, never baked documentary graphics.
- No exact Aku-01 location, named host community, oil-company employment, field PPE, witness framing, guaranteed job, invented spill or benefit.
- Generate still continuity first. A moving promo is a later separate approval and must not be inferred from still approval.
