← PerspectivesMINERALS + RESOURCES
01 · COMPUTE

The question feels weightless. The system answering it is not.

AI models run inside physical infrastructure: servers, storage, networking and cooling. The IEA estimates data centres used about 415 TWh of electricity in 2024, around 1.5% of global electricity consumption.

Evidence · IEA · SR-01–03
02 · GRID + MATERIALS

More computation reaches beyond the server rack.

Growing capacity needs electricity connections, transformers, substations, cables and other grid equipment. Copper is one of the materials used across data centres and the wider electricity system.

Evidence · IEA · SR-02–04
03 · GEOGRAPHY

A global demand system becomes local somewhere.

Copper is not an “AI metal.” AI and data-centre growth enters a much larger demand system spanning grids, buildings, transport, industry and electrification.

Boundary · connection, not singular causation
04 · CHILE

The chain narrows toward a major copper-producing country.

Chile is one of the world’s major copper producers. Within Chile, Antofagasta is the dominant production centre; Cochilco projected it at 58% of national copper production in 2025.

Evidence · Cochilco · SR-05–06
05 · ANTOFAGASTA

Desert, coast, cities and mines share one regional system.

This establishes a place where global material demand meets an existing copper economy. It does not establish that AI caused a particular mine expansion.

Evidence boundary preserved
06 · CALAMA / CHUQUICAMATA

The material chain arrives beside a real city.

Chuquicamata lies about 15 km north of Calama. Codelco reports 266 thousand tonnes of fine copper produced in 2025 and 3,992 direct employees at year end. Calama itself is more than a mine: it is a city of households, schools, businesses and ordinary life.

Evidence · Codelco + UCN · SR-07–12
ATLASOQUENCE BOUNDARY

What this perspective does — and does not — establish.

AI/data-centre growth adds pressure inside a much larger electricity and copper demand system. This route does not claim AI caused an existing Antofagasta mine expansion, a particular job, or the fictional events in the Daniela and Mauricio story.
