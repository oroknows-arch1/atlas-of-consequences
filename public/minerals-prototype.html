03 / 05 · GEOGRAPHY

Follow copper to Chile.

AI did not create copper demand. It enters an established global copper system as an additional source of infrastructure demand. Chile is a major part of that existing system.

THE EVIDENCE STOPS HERE

Connection is not causation.

This path does not establish that AI caused Chuquicamata, a particular Chilean mining investment or a local employment outcome. It shows where additional infrastructure demand can meet an older, concentrated copper economy.
