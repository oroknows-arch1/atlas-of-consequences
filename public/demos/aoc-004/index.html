01 · What's real

A reported failure. An unresolved cause.

Supplied account — independently uncorroborated. An unidentified operator reportedly engaged a civil contractor to prepare an onshore drilling location in Nigeria’s eastern Niger Delta. After a drilling contractor mobilised, the pad reportedly failed during pulling out of hole for a wiper trip. The rig partially sank, the location was evacuated, and the pad and rig were reportedly replaced. No primary incident record is available.

REPORTED

Pad failure, partial rig sinking, evacuation, reconstruction and rig replacement.

UNRESOLVED

Design basis, ground conditions, construction quality, loads, acceptance and causal mechanism.

NOT ESTABLISHED

Injury, pollution, loss of well control, negligence, liability or wider community loss.
