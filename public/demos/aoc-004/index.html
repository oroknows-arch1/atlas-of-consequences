What do we actually know?

SUPPLIED ACCOUNT — uncorroborated

A major operator engaged a local civil contractor to construct a drilling pad in the onshore Eastern Niger Delta. A Chinese drilling contractor mobilised and began drilling. During pulling out of hole for a wiper trip, the pad reportedly failed and the rig partially sank. Evacuation, pad reconstruction and rig replacement were reported.

UNRESOLVED — mechanisms and responsibility

The actual design, site tests, as-built quality records, rig loads, acceptance documents and investigation are not available. No party's liability or failure mechanism is established.

UNKNOWN — additional outcomes

Any injury, well-control event, pollution, local economic effect or warning prior to the failure is unknown. Silence is not evidence that these occurred or did not occur.

COMPARATIVE RESEARCH — not evidence of this event

Niger Delta drilling-platform foundation case history (2013); IADC model drilling contracts. Both help frame questions, neither verifies AOC-004.

Three different acceptance questions

G1 — Built to specification? Construction records and test results.

G2 — Fit for this rig? Engineering checks using actual rig loads and the site's ground conditions.

G3 — Ready to drill? Operational readiness and any unresolved exceptions.

These are analytical categories, not verified formal handover gates in the actual project.
