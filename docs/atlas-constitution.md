# Atlas of Consequences — Constitution

## Purpose
Atlas of Consequences is a global storytelling and publishing system downstream from EMRADAR.

EMRADAR discovers consequential real-world change. Atlas explores what those changes could mean for people and places. OROK provides surrounding educational, cultural, and human context.

**Guiding line:** Global consequences. Local voices. Common human threads.

## Constitutional rule
**Facts can change the fiction. Fiction must never quietly become fact.**

Readers must always be able to distinguish sourced real-world information from fictional narrative.

## Locality principle
Atlas stories should feel informed by the place from the inside and credible to readers familiar with that country or region.

Before story generation, build a Local Context Pack from appropriate evidence: local and regional reporting, original local-language sources, government/statistical information, universities and research, community organisations, geography, employment and industries, housing, family/community structures, history, terminology, cultural context, and current local debates.

Translation may support research, but original sources remain traceable.

Do not build characters from stereotypes.

**Research the stereotype; write the individual.**

Universal OROK themes may connect stories globally — family, work, responsibility, knowledge, land, ambition, belonging, change, and intergenerational legacy — while their expression remains local.

## Publishing boundary
Atlas is a publishing product, not merely a story generator.

Each complete edition can contain:
1. REALITY — sourced explanation of what was discovered.
2. STORY — clearly labelled fiction exploring plausible human consequences.
3. CONSEQUENCES — the evidence-aware chain from development to downstream effects.
4. PLACE / CONTEXT — locality material needed for understanding.
5. SOURCES — original evidence and links.
6. OROK COMPANION — educational and cultural material around human themes.
7. DISTRIBUTION — social/media derivatives pointing back to the permanent Atlas edition.

## v0.1 rule
Prove one complete edition before scaling the system.

One ecosystem. One locality. One complete story experience. Visible evidence. Clear fact/fiction boundary. OROK companion material. One complete social Publishing Package.
